## 1. What HIVE Doctor is
HIVE Doctor is an **educational reference tool**. It organises general medical information so you can learn and prepare better questions. It is a reference, like a well-made book — not a professional, and not a substitute for one.

## 2. Not medical advice; no professional relationship
Nothing in HIVE Doctor is medical advice. Using it does **not** create a clinician–client relationship. It does not know your full situation, cannot examine you or your documents, and cannot be responsible for decisions you make. The content has not yet been independently verified by a licensed professional; it is the author's own reference work, provided for education.

## 3. In an emergency
If you or someone else may be seriously ill or injured, call your local emergency number now. Do not wait, and do not rely on HIVE Doctor in an emergency. If someone may have taken too much of a medicine or a poison, call Poison Control (US and Canada 1-800-222-1222) or your emergency number. If someone may be thinking of harming themselves, call or text a crisis line (988 in the US and Canada) or emergency services.

## 7. Privacy
HIVE Doctor runs entirely on your own device and works offline. It does **not** send your questions, your medicine list, your documents, or anything you type to the author or any server. Anything it remembers (such as a medicine list) is stored only in your own browser on your own device and never leaves it. If you publish HIVE Doctor to a web host, that host may keep its own access logs — review the host's policy.
