What it is

HIVE Doctor is an educational reference that runs entirely on your device. Ask it about a symptom, a medicine, or a condition and it answers from a fixed, tested body of knowledge — then shows its sources. It reads photos of letters and reports without internet, tracks your medicines for interactions, and holds a medical book deep enough that a specialist would recognise their own field in it.

What it does

Everything runs on your own device, offline, with no account and no data leaving the machine.

Symptom reasoning

A deterministic triage engine weighs your symptoms, flags red-flags and emergencies first, and never fabricates a diagnosis.

Medicine reference

573 medicines drawn verbatim from official US labels, most with a package picture, each with its label date and a link. Dose text is reference only.

Interaction tracker

Tell it what you take and it flags well-known serious interactions, duplicates, grapefruit and food timing — and says plainly which medicines it has no rules for.

A real medical book

68 chapters from emergency and surgery to psychiatry, oncology, genetics and newborn care — each tested against a board-style concept list.

Picture atlas

Original labelled diagrams plus hundreds of real example photographs, every one carrying its author and licence.

Reads your documents

Scan a photo of a lab report or letter and it reads and explains it — with the text-reader bundled, so it works with no connection.

Teaches and quizzes

Ask it to explain any chapter, or to quiz you on it.

Yours, offline

No account, no cloud, no tracking. Everything stays on your device and keeps working with the internet off.

Who it's for

  • Working professionals. A fast, offline second reference that covers neighbouring specialties as well as your own.
  • Students. A board-level body of knowledge you can read, be quizzed on, and carry anywhere.
  • People without easy access to care. Plain-language answers, red-flag warnings, and a clear push to real care when it matters.

Where it stands — honestly

HIVE Doctor is content-complete and every automated gate passes — reproducibility, a per-specialty board of 922 concepts, hardening, proofreading of 5,600 passages, picture-fit, and 165 in-browser checks including emergencies and adversarial safety questions. It is not yet signed off by a clinician.

  • The content has not yet been independently verified by a licensed physician; a sign-off checklist is included for professional review.
  • It is a learning tool, not a doctor, and not a substitute for care.
  • Medicine labels are US-only; some medicines have no interaction rules yet, and those are disclosed in the app.
  • Photographs are examples; conditions look different on different bodies and a photo cannot diagnose.
