Paste a medical letter, lab report, or discharge summary — or scan a photo of one — and HIVE reads it into plain language: a point-form breakdown, the values and follow-ups worth asking about, and a jargon translator. It explains, it does not diagnose.

📊 Track & graph
Chart readings over time — blood pressure, weight, blood sugar, a symptom score (0–10), anything. Enter values separated by commas, or one per line as label: value. A reference band shows a typical healthy range where relevant.
A tracking aid, not a diagnosis. Share trends with your doctor — one reading rarely tells the story.
