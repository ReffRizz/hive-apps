# HIVE Doctor — reviewer sign-off checklist

**Purpose.** The content in HIVE Doctor has not yet been independently verified by a licensed professional (status *UNVERIFIED*). This document is the controlled way to change that. A **licensed physician (with specialists for specialist chapters)** works through each chapter, records corrections, and signs. Nothing in HIVE Doctor should be described to users as reviewed, verified, or professional until the sign-off block at the end of this file is completed.

This checklist is not itself medical advice and does not create any professional relationship.

## How to use it
1. Work one chapter at a time. Read it in full against a current, authoritative source you trust.
2. For each chapter mark the three checks (accuracy, currency, safety) only when you are satisfied.
3. Record every correction in the Corrections log below, or send a marked-up copy back to the builder. **Do not** mark a chapter signed if any correction is still outstanding.
4. When — and only when — every chapter is signed and every correction is closed, complete the final sign-off block. The builder then changes each chapter's `review_status` from UNVERIFIED to the reviewed wording you approve, and updates the in-app status line.

## The three checks for every chapter
- **Accuracy** — the statements are correct and not misleading as written.
- **Currency** — the statements reflect current guidelines and practice; note anything that has moved on.
- **Safety** — the red-flags, cautions and "when to get urgent care" are present, correct, and not likely to lull a reader into a false sense of security.

## Scope note for this reviewer
- Dose numbers: the chapters deliberately contain **no dose figures**. Dosing appears only as verbatim excerpts from official US product labels in the medicine reference, each with its label date and a link. Please confirm this separation has held and that no chapter has acquired a dose figure.
- Medicine reference: 573 medicines are label-sourced. Please spot-check that excerpts match the linked label and that the "reference only — not a dosing guide" framing is intact.
- Interaction tracker: 290 of 500 medicines are mapped to interaction classes with 103 rules. Please confirm the flagged interactions are correct and that unclassified medicines are honestly disclosed as not-checked.
- Photographs: 379 example images carry author + licence (see PHOTO_CREDITS.md). Please flag any image that is mislabelled or clinically misleading, especially across skin tones.
- Emergencies & self-harm: please stress-test the red-flag, overdose, poisoning and crisis responses.
