What it is

HIVE Lawyer is an educational reference that runs entirely on your device. Describe a problem and it names the area of law, the deadlines that bite, your rights, and when you truly need a lawyer — citing as it goes. Its Letter Lab reads a letter, breaks it into points, flags what's hurried or wrong, and drafts a measured reply. It carries a legal book deep enough to take a field's bar seriously.

Where it stands — honestly

HIVE Lawyer is content-complete and every automated gate passes — reproducibility, a per-specialty bar of 917 concepts, hardening, proofreading of 20,000+ passages, picture-fit, an AI-safety guard, and 37 in-browser checks. All 58 case citations were checked. It is not yet signed off by a lawyer.

  • The content has not yet been independently verified by a lawyer; a sign-off checklist is included for professional review.
  • It is a learning tool, not a lawyer, and does not give advice for your specific situation.
  • Case citations were checked against general legal knowledge, not formally noted up.
  • Primary-source verification of deadlines and statutes is partial and tracked openly in the build.
